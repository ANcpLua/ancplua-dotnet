# MSTest Extensions privacy policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

MSTest Extensions is maintained by Alexander Nachtmann (ANcpLua). This
policy covers this plugin's skills, supporting scripts and repository support.

## Plugin operation

The plugin supplies instructions and local helper scripts for native MSTest and
Microsoft.Testing.Platform workflows. It has no publisher-operated backend,
account system, advertising trackers or built-in usage analytics. Installing it
does not give the maintainer access to your repositories, prompts or test results.

When you use the plugin, your AI host may process prompts, source code, file
paths, package versions, build logs, test results and other context supplied by
you or made available to the host. That material may contain personal or
confidential information. Processing, storage, retention and any model-training
choices are governed by your host's policies, account settings and agreements.
For ChatGPT and Codex, see [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

The bundled scripts can inspect project and package metadata, invoke your .NET
tools, restore dependencies and write diagnostic reports or verification
artifacts in your execution environment. Restores can contact NuGet feeds;
other requested workflows may consult documentation or services. Those services
receive the information needed for the request, such as package identifiers,
versions and ordinary network connection information, under their own policies.
The plugin does not contain a mechanism that sends your workspace to its maintainer.

## Support information

If you open a GitHub issue, discussion or pull request, you choose what to share.
Your GitHub identity, message and attachments may be visible to the maintainer
and the public. The maintainer uses that information to respond, investigate
problems and maintain the plugin; it is not used for advertising or sold.
Do not post credentials, private source code or personal information in public
support requests. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Retention and controls

The maintainer keeps no separate store of your prompts, workspace or test runs.
Local reports remain where your tools or the scripts write them until you remove
them. Your AI host and external services control retention for their copies.
Public GitHub contributions can remain available in the repository and its
history until removed; third-party caches or copies may persist.

You can disable or uninstall the plugin, limit the files and commands available
to your host, change the host's data controls, and delete local artifacts. For
support-content correction or removal, use GitHub's available controls or ask
the maintainer through the [issue tracker](https://github.com/ANcpLua/mstest-extensions-plugin/issues),
without including sensitive details. Requests concerning a host or service's
records should be directed to that provider.

## Questions and changes

Privacy questions can be raised through the same issue tracker. Policy updates
are published in this repository with an updated effective date. This policy
does not replace the privacy policies of OpenAI, GitHub, NuGet or your other tools.
